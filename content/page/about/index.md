## The Sage Behind the Guides

I'm Aestus, and I've been playing computer RPGs for over twenty years. What started with the Baldur's Gate series in the late '90s turned into a lifelong obsession with the systems that make these games tick --- the math behind the magic, the builds behind the victories, the design decisions that separate a great RPG from a good one.

Aestus Guides is where I put that experience to work. Every guide on this site comes from hundreds of hours of play, testing, and analysis. When I write a tier list, I explain my methodology first. When I recommend a build, I show you exactly how to construct it. I don't do clickbait and I don't do shallow --- if you're here, you're the kind of player who wants to understand *why* something works, not just that it does.

## What You'll Find Here

**Tier Lists & Guides** --- Deep analytical breakdowns of classes, companions, weapons, and builds across games like Baldur's Gate 3, Pillars of Eternity, and Pathfinder. These aren't quick rankings --- they're full analyses with reasoning, build outlines, and honest acknowledgment of subjectivity.

**System Analysis** --- Articles exploring the fundamental math and design principles behind CRPG combat systems. How initiative actually works. Why some multiclass combinations are mathematically superior. The hidden numbers that shape every encounter.

**Interviews** --- Conversations with game designers and creators in the CRPG and tabletop RPG space, exploring the craft behind the games we love.

**Pathfinder Journals** --- A veteran RPG player's honest account of diving into a new system for the first time, documenting the learning curve, the surprises, and the builds along the way.

## The Name

*Aestus* is Latin for heat, passion, fervor. It felt right for a site driven by genuine enthusiasm for these games --- not obligation, not content farming, but the kind of deep engagement that comes from truly loving a genre.

*Memoirs of a CRPG Sage* --- because after twenty-plus years in these worlds, you accumulate a lot of stories, a lot of knowledge, and a lot of opinions worth sharing.
